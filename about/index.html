Littleton Slab Leak Pros is a Littleton, Colorado company that focuses on one thing: finding and fixing leaks under concrete floors. We've been doing it since 2015, from our shop at 6070 S Sycamore St, and we've completed more than 3,600 jobs for homeowners across Littleton.

Why focus only on slab leaks?

A general plumber handles everything from faucets to water heaters. Slab leaks are different: they're hidden, the stakes are high, and the biggest cost driver is how accurately the leak is found. By focusing on detection and slab leak repair, we've built the equipment, experience and judgment to find leaks faster and recommend the right fix — not the biggest one.

How we work

  • Detection before demolition. We confirm and pinpoint every leak before recommending any concrete be opened.
  • Every option, priced in writing. You'll see the cheapest repair that will work alongside longer-term options.
  • Honest about trade-offs. If a spot repair is likely to be followed by another leak, we'll say so. If a reroute is overkill, we'll say that too.
  • Respect for your home. Floor protection, dust containment and clean-up are part of every job.
  • Always open. We answer 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, because slab leaks don't wait for business hours.

What we know about Littleton homes

Littleton is a mix of century-old downtown homes, mid-century ranches and split-levels, 1970s and 1980s neighborhoods built around parks and greenbelts, and newer communities to the south and west. Clay soils across the area move with moisture, and much of the housing has copper lines under basement or garage slabs that are now decades old. That combination shapes almost every job we do — and it's why our first question is always "when was your home built?"

What to expect when you call us

You'll talk to someone who understands slab leaks. They'll ask a few quick questions, tell you what to shut off if water is surfacing, and schedule a technician — or send one right away in an emergency. You'll hear about any after-hours fee before anyone heads out.

Our commitment on price

You'll always get a written price before work begins, and if what we find on site changes the plan, we stop and talk with you before going further. No surprises, ever. That's how we've worked since 2015.
