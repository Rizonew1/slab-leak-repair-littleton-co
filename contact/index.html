Before you call, it helps to know

  • Roughly when your home was built
  • What you're noticing — high bill, warm spot, sound of water, damp floor
  • Whether water is surfacing or you can stop it
  • Whether you've had slab leaks before
Emergency tip: If water is coming up through the floor, shut off the main valve and your water heater first, then call.

What happens after you call

  1. We ask a few questions and give you any emergency steps.
  2. We schedule detection, or dispatch right away for emergencies.
  3. A technician locates the leak and gives you written repair options.

Water bill climbing or a warm spot on the floor?

Every hour a slab leak runs, more water goes into the soil under your home. Call now and we'll send a technician with leak-locating gear, day or night.

Is your situation an emergency?

If water is surfacing through the floor, you see steam or feel a very hot floor, or a lower-level drain is backing up, treat it as an emergency and call immediately. If it's a high bill or a meter that keeps moving, it's still worth calling today — slab leaks grow over time, and early detection usually means a smaller, cheaper repair.
